1.What product are you planning to build?
We are planning to build CyberShield, a cybersecurity app that helps users recover hacked accounts and improve online safety.
2.What problem does it solve?
It helps users who do not know how to respond when their accounts are hacked or how to protect their personal information.
3.Who are the target users?
Our target users include individuals, students, businesses, organizations, and anyone who uses online accounts, digital services, or computer systems and needs help with cybersecurity.
4.What is the main use case?
Users can follow step-by-step instructions when they suspect their accounts have been hacked.
5.What are the core features?
Interactive security questions, account recovery guidance, possible causes of security incidents, and privacy-setting recommendations.
6.What assumptions are you making about the users?
We assume users need simple instructions to understand security problems and protect their online accounts.
7.What will be included in your MVP?
A security questionnaire, account recovery checklists, and guidance on important security settings.
8.What will NOT be included in the MVP?
Automatic hacking detection, antivirus scanning, and automatic changes to account settings.
